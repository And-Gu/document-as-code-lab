# Onboarding Handbook

Fictional review copy. Includes draft material; not approved for operational use.

## Requesting Access

Owner: onboarding-team. Status: approved.

Submit an access request through the service portal. State which system you need and which team you belong to. Keep the confirmation reference number and include it when asking the service desk about progress.

## Collecting Equipment

Owner: workplace-team. Status: approved.

Arrange an equipment handover with the workplace team. Check the supplied items against the handover list and report anything missing.

## Finding Team Contacts

Owner: people-team. Status: draft.

Ask the people team for the current contact directory. Confirm your manager and the contact responsible for onboarding questions.
